# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a useful, respectful place for practitioners across disciplines and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal worth.
- Be precise, constructive, and willing to correct mistakes.
- Respect different risk contexts, legal environments, accessibility needs, and operational constraints.
- Protect confidential, personal, and security-sensitive information.
- Give credit for others’ work and sources.
- Make space for people with less familiarity with a standard or practice.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing private or sensitive information are not acceptable. Neither is using the project to encourage unauthorized testing or harm.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a repository security advisory when no other private contact is published. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

Maintainers should handle reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain enforcement decisions when it is safe and appropriate to do so.
